## Introduction
My name is **Nguyen Viet Khanh**, a senior student in the Automation and Control Program at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST). With a strong academic background and hands-on research experience in motion control for mobile robots and intelligent control strategies, I am eager to pursue graduate studies at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)** in the **Control, Intelligent Systems, and Robotics (CISR)** group. My long-term goal is to contribute to cutting-edge research in control systems and intelligent robotics, integrating machine learning and advanced control methodologies to develop robust and adaptive autonomous systems.

## My Journey into Engineering

Growing up in Hanoi, I developed an early fascination with control engineering. My first encounter was at the **2010 Honda Conference**, where I witnessed the **ASIMO humanoid robot**. Its ability to perform tasks and interact with humans seemed like something out of science fiction, igniting my passion for robotics and intelligent systems.

While I consistently performed well academically, I faced challenges in my early education. My school lacked advanced resources, and I initially doubted my potential to succeed as an engineer. A pivotal moment came when I failed the entrance exam for specialized schools in the city. However, I quickly prepared for the independent entrance exam at **Nguyen Tat Thanh High School** and secured admission to a competitive special class. Though I started behind my peers, I worked diligently and surprised myself by excelling, ultimately discovering my untapped potential.

In 2021, I began my undergraduate journey at the **School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering** at HUST. Transitioning to university during the COVID-19 pandemic was a challenging experience, but I adapted quickly. I joined the **Mechatronic Engineering Group (MEG)** to gain hands-on experience in research and control systems. This experience cemented my decision to dedicate myself fully to this field, a choice I consider one of the most fulfilling of my academic career. During this time, I explored advanced concepts and engaged in self-study, often staying up until 2 a.m., fueled by curiosity. This passion for learning resulted in several publications in prestigious journals and international conferences. I am eager to pursue a **Master of Engineering** at NUS in the **CISR research group**.

## Research and Practical Experience

My undergraduate research began with designing a control architecture for a 3-omni-wheeled robot, utilizing **Sliding Mode Control** for its nonlinear dynamics and implementing the **A*** algorithm for path planning. Although I did not publish this work due to limited novelty, it provided a solid foundation in control systems and algorithm implementation. Under the supervision of **Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Tung Lam**, my main undergraduate project focused on controlling **liquid sloshing dynamics**. I explored advanced techniques such as fixed-time control, predictive control, and optimal control. My most significant contribution was proving the **non-flatness properties** of liquid sloshing objects and introducing an estimated flat output for trajectory generation and control — a novel approach in the field. While practical experiments were constrained by sensor limitations, I implemented a feedforward control system to guide a container along a reference trajectory using a servo motor.

In addition, I gained practical experience through smaller projects, including **DC motor control** using STM32 microcontrollers and a **self-balancing robot** using LabVIEW and the myRIO controller. Although the self-balancing robot is still a work in progress, these hands-on projects have sharpened my technical and problem-solving skills.

In the next semester, I will have an internship at the **Computational Intelligence and Robotics Lab** at **National Taiwan Normal University (NTNU)**, working as a research student. I expect to gain valuable experience in developing **multi-objective crowd-aware robot navigation systems** using **deep reinforcement learning**. This opportunity will allow me to enhance my skills in **ROS** and **Python**, while also experiencing an international tech ecosystem and diverse academic culture. I look forward to engaging with leading researchers and gaining insights into real-world applications of autonomous navigation in complex environments.

## Research Interests and Future Goals

As I look toward my future in the field of Automatic Control at the Department of Electrical Engineering, I am particularly drawn to the intersection of **Robust Control**, **Predictive Control**, and **Neural Networks**. My undergraduate research experiences have instilled in me a strong passion for **Model Predictive Control (MPC)** and **Sliding Mode Control** techniques for autonomous systems, as they provide a powerful framework for addressing uncertainties and constraints in dynamic environments.  

However, my scientific journey has just begun, and I long to learn new methods in Automatic Control. Furthermore, my growing curiosity for **Deep Learning** and **Reinforcement Learning** has opened up a new dimension of possibilities for integrating machine learning with traditional control methodologies. I am eager to explore how these advanced techniques can be harnessed to enhance the adaptability, decision-making capabilities, and overall efficiency of robotic systems operating in real-world conditions.

The **Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)** at **NUS** is globally recognized for its excellence in research and innovation. The **CISR group’s** expertise in precision motion control, robust system design, and AI-driven robotics makes it the ideal place for me to further develop my knowledge. Additionally, NUS's strong collaborations with leading research institutions and industries, such as **SIMTech**, **Hexagon**, **Seagate Automation**, **Siemens**, and **Yokogawa**, provide a dynamic environment for translating research into real-world applications.

Furthermore, the structure of the graduate program at NUS, with its emphasis on both theoretical foundations and hands-on applications, will enable me to refine my research skills while also engaging in interdisciplinary collaborations. I am particularly interested in courses related to **nonlinear systems**, **adaptive control**, and **computational intelligence**, which will complement my existing expertise and allow me to develop innovative control strategies for complex engineering systems.

Ultimately, I envision a future where I can contribute to the advancement of intelligent autonomous systems through research and development. After completing the **Master's Program in Automatic Control**, I plan to pursue a **PhD** to further refine my expertise and delve deeper into my research interests. My ultimate goal is to become an **R&D control expert**, leveraging my skills to pioneer cutting-edge technologies in robotics and control engineering. I believe that after my PhD, I could make meaningful contributions to production lines in practice, not just in theory. This journey, beginning with the **Master of Engineering Program at NUS**, will equip me with the technical expertise, innovative mindset, and global perspective necessary to address critical challenges in the field and make a meaningful impact on the global engineering community.
